❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to common questions about Casa Liezl Private Resort.

1. Allowed ba ang magdala ng outside food and drinks? May corkage fee ba? ▼
Yes! Feel free to bring your own food, drinks, and grilling items. Absolutely NO CORKAGE FEE for regular food and non-alcoholic beverages.
2. Ano ang eksaktong Check-in at Check-out schedule? ▼
Our standard shift schedules are:
• Day Tour: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
• Night Tour: 7:00 PM to 5:00 AM
• 22-Hour Exclusive Stay: Complete 22 hours private staycation package.

After sending your GCash downpayment to our official account, our resort team will verify your transaction and update your reservation status instantly.
