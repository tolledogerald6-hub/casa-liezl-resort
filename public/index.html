❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to common questions about Casa Liezl Resort.

1. Allowed ba ang magdala ng outside food and drinks? May corkage fee ba? ▼
Yes! Feel free to bring your own food, drinks, and grilling items. Absolutely NO CORKAGE FEE for regular food and non-alcoholic beverages.
2. Ano ang eksaktong Check-in at Check-out schedule? ▼
Our standard shift schedules are:
• Day Tour: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
• Night Tour: 7:00 PM to 5:00 AM
• 24-Hour Overnight: Flexible 24-hour full stay staycation.
3. Paano i-confirm ang reservation matapos mag-submit ng form? ▼
After sending your GCash downpayment reference number, our resort team will verify your transaction and update your reservation status. You can also download your official PDF Voucher instantly!
